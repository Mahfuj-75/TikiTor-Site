How Tikitor Works

From finding an event to walking in with a QR code, in four steps.

1

Find an event

Search by event, artist or city. Filter by Concert, Comedy, Sports or what’s on this week.

2

Create an account

Sign in to save events to your wishlist and keep every booking in one place.

3

Book and get your ticket

Pay online. Your mobile ticket appears in My tickets with a Valid status and a QR code.

4

Scan to enter

Show your QR code at the entrance. It is scanned once and you walk in.

Before you go

Important notes

  • Arrive 30 minutes before the event.
  • Show your valid QR code at the entrance.
  • One ticket is valid for one person.
  • Keep your ticket safe.

Ticket policy

  • Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Duplicate or invalid tickets will not be accepted.
  • QR codes can be scanned only once.
  • Follow all event rules and organizer instructions.

From idea to full house

1

Create your event

Fill in the five steps and save it as a draft.

2

Submit for review

Organizers cannot publish directly. Your event goes live after Tikitor Admin approves it. If changes are needed, you will see Changes requested.

3

Sell and check in

Sell tickets online and on-site. Guests pay by bKash, Nagad, card or cash. Scan them in at the gate.

4

Get paid

Add your payout details and request a withdrawal when you are ready.

Trusted and Transparent

Verified organizers

Upload your TIN certificate, trade license and event permission. Files are private: only you and Tikitor Admin can see them (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB).

Event readiness

A checklist shows what is left before you are ready: event details and banner, tickets, Admin approval, payout details and event terms.
